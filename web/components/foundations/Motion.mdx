# Motion

One curve and two durations for the whole site, kept in one place: `lib/motion/tokens.ts` for Motion
(`EASE_SMOOTH`, `DURATION_SHORT`, `DURATION_LONG`) and the same three as CSS variables in `app/globals.css`
(`--ease-smooth`, `--duration-short`, `--duration-long`; the utilities `ease-smooth`, `duration-short`,
`duration-long`). A test fails if the two ever differ, and another if a component types in a curve or a duration of
its own.

The curve is one bezier (WQ88, which replaces the spring of WQ55 and WQ56): off at once and fast, then a long, soft
settling, never past its target. An animation of the short duration plays the same shape in half the time. Its four
numbers are written in two places only, the stylesheet's token and `lib/motion/tokens.ts`, and shown under the plot
below.

- **Short**: hovers, and the menu.
- **Long**: page transitions, each of the intro's three transitions, and the Product Info Panel's slide.

The intro also has two pauses of 480 ms (`--intro-pause`, `INTRO_PAUSE`): waits between its transitions, not a
third duration.

Smooth scroll is Lenis, started once for the whole site. A panel that scrolls by itself (the menu, the bag, the
booking panel) carries `data-lenis-prevent`, or Lenis takes its scrolling for the page behind it.
